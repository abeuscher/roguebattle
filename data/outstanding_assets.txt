OUTSTANDING ASSETS
==================
What the game still needs from you, and where each file goes. When a file lands, tell
Claude: it gets trimmed, levelled and wired in, and the line here comes off.
(Updated 2026-11-16.)


NEEDED
------

Battle: the support-magic sounds
  Folder: data/audio/battle_src/
  Files:  buff.wav     a unit strengthens an ally (Defence up, Agility up, Magic defence up)
          debuff.wav   a unit weakens an enemy (Strength down, Agility down, poison and the like)
          ward.wav     a protective spell (Ward attacks)
          dispel.wav   a spell that strips buffs or curses (Dispel attacks)
  Any length; the game shortens battle sounds to 1.2 seconds so they don't run over the
  next action at fast speeds. Until these are in, all four borrow the heal sound.


MAYBE (only if you decide you want them)
----------------------------------------

Voice: an enemy squad wiped out
  Folder: data/audio/voice/
  A short line for when an enemy squad is wiped out and leaves the board. Not decided.

Extra takes, for variety
  Folder: next to the file it varies (for example data/audio/voice/victory_2.wav)
  A second or third take of a sound that plays often (the victory fanfare, "Victory!",
  "Defeat!", battle hits), so the game never plays the same take twice in a row. The
  game can't pick between takes yet; that gets built once there are takes to pick from.


PLACEHOLDERS YOU MAY REPLACE
----------------------------
These play now, made by Claude; swap in your own any time.

Map ambience (the background sound under the map's music)
  Folder: data/audio/ambience_src/day/, .../night/ and .../any/ (any time of day)
  A looping bed per time of day (a file whose name starts with "bed") and short one-shots
  (birds, wind, distant bells...). One-shots named for a condition only play when it's
  true: hooves (a mounted squad moving in sight), waves (near the sea), river (beside one).
  See data/audio/ambience_src/README.md.

Battle build-up music (before a fight, while the warning is up)
  File: data/audio/music/battle_intro.wav (a MIDI draft; the source is
  data/audio/midi/battle_intro.mid)
